
## Research checkpoint 1
- Los Baños municipal history source: https://losbanos.gov.ph/abouts — identifies the settlement’s hot-spring origin, the 1589 public baths associated with Fr. Pedro Bautista, and historical context connected to Mt. Makiling, José Rizal, and Apolinario Mabini.
- Los Baños municipal profile: https://losbanos.gov.ph/municipal_hall — describes Los Baños as a municipality on Mt. Makiling’s northern slopes, known for hot-spring resorts, universities, research centers, and native delicacies such as buko pie.
- UPLB visitor search results identify Makiling Botanic Garden as a 300-hectare living laboratory and museum of endemic/tropical plants, and UPLB’s tourist-spot page lists Sining Makiling Gallery, Parasite Collection Center, National Institute of Molecular Biology and Biotechnology, National Crop Protection Center, and Mud Spring. Direct UPLB pages were behind a challenge page, so access hours are not asserted.
- UPLB Museum of Natural History homepage: https://mnh.uplb.edu.ph/ — institutional identity and Los Baños/UPLB location confirmed; access details remain to be verified.
- IRRI: https://www.irri.org/ — official headquarters at Pili Drive, Los Baños; official site includes “Experience IRRI” visitor/experiential-tour information and supports an education/science category, but no opening hours are asserted.
- Research results also surfaced Mount Makiling, Flat Rocks, Dampalit Falls, UPLB Fertility Tree, Shrine of St. Therese of the Child Jesus, Roman Catholic Parish of San Antonio de Padua, Olivarez Plaza, and Japanese Garden/Steps as candidate records. Japanese Garden needs boundary verification because it may be outside Los Baños proper.
